# Project Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- **FR-01 (Invitation Screen)**: Scene 01 opening experience with starry backdrop, floating gift illustration, headline "Hey, Vale.", and button "Open your little surprise" that unlocks the main voyage.
- **FR-02 (Editorial Introduction)**: Scene 02 typography highlight on "VALERIE" with friendship dedication and subtle scroll indicator.
- **FR-03 (Digital Scrapbook Gallery)**: Scene 03 Polaroid cards with subtle rotation, film grain, paper textures, handwritten captions, and full Lightbox modal viewing using images from `/images` (`image 1.png`, `image 2.png`, `image 3.png`).
- **FR-04 (Interactive Appreciation Cards)**: Scene 04 interactive cards ("Your kind of chaos", "Things that make you, you", "Your underrated superpowers", "Reasons you're appreciated") revealing custom messages on flip/click.
- **FR-05 (Editorial Letter)**: Scene 05 letter with warm personalized text, classic stationery presentation, and wax seal motif.
- **FR-06 (3D Surprise Box)**: Scene 06 interactive gift box where clicking lifts the lid, bursts sparkling particles, and reveals the hidden birthday message ("ONE MORE YEAR OF BEING WONDERFULLY YOU").
- **FR-07 (Grand Finale & Cake)**: Scene 07 celebration with heading "HAPPY BIRTHDAY, VALE!", interactive birthday cake with candle that can be blown out, confetti cannon, replay button, and footer "Made with care, just for Vale."
- **FR-08 (Ambient Audio)**: Optional ambient soundtrack toggle with audio state indicator and Web Audio API celestial pad synthesizer.
- **FR-09 (Centralized Content)**: Centralized content store (`src/data/birthdayContent.ts`) for easily modifying words and photos.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **NFR-01 (Mobile First)**: Fully responsive across iPhone, Android, tablet, and desktop viewports.
- **NFR-02 (Design Consistency)**: Strict adherence to color palette (Warm Ivory, Dusty Rose, Muted Lavender, Deep Plum, Soft Gold) and typography rules.
- **NFR-03 (Performance & Polish)**: Fluid 60fps animations via Framer Motion, zero layout shifts, zero console errors.
