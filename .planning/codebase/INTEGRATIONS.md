# Integrations & External Assets

## Integrations
1. **Google Fonts**:
   - `Cormorant Garamond` (weights 400, 600, 700)
   - `DM Sans` (weights 400, 500, 700)
   - `Caveat` (weights 400, 700)
   Imported via `<link rel="preconnect">` in `index.html` for zero-layout-shift rendering.

2. **Canvas Confetti**:
   - High-performance canvas-based particle physics for realistic celebratory confetti explosions.

3. **Web Audio API**:
   - Zero-dependency client-side procedural soundscape synthesizer. Generates lush pentatonic chimes and warm ambient celestial pads when sound is toggled on, guaranteeing that ambient sound works immediately without requiring heavy audio assets or risking missing files.

4. **Local Photography**:
   - Integrates user-provided images from `src/images/`:
     - `image 1.png`
     - `image 2.png`
     - `image 3.png`
